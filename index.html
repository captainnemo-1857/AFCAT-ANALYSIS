09

Methodology & honest limitations

Source: 24 unique papers (22 previous-year memory-based/official reconstructions, 1 compiled solved paper, plus 2 model papers analysed separately) spanning AFCAT 01/2021 through AFCAT 02/2026. Two duplicate files and three redundant compiled-paper duplicates were dropped before analysis.

Extraction: Text pulled directly from each PDF. Two 2025 papers were scanned images and required OCR (Tesseract) — expect a slightly higher error rate there. One 2024 paper had a multi-column layout that scrambled reading order; its four subject sections were recovered reliably from header positions, but individual question boundaries within each section are approximate.

Subject and topic tagging: Done with a rule-based keyword classifier, not by hand and not by an LLM reading every question — this was a deliberate choice to keep the method transparent and auditable rather than a black box. It was calibrated against the one paper with genuine printed section headers (English/Maths/Reasoning/GK), and its output distribution lands within a few percentage points of that ground truth across all four subjects. About 30% of questions fell back to secondary heuristics (structural cues like body length and numeric density) rather than a direct keyword hit — these are individually less reliable, but in aggregate the topic-share trends held up under manual spot-checking of ~150+ questions across the corpus.

GK's "Other/Miscellaneous" bucket stays large deliberately — general awareness questions span an enormous range of one-off facts, and force-fitting them into narrow categories would manufacture false precision.

Difficulty proxy is question length only. It does not account for conceptual difficulty, distractor quality, or actual candidate performance data (which isn't public). Treat the difficulty chart as a read on verbosity/time-pressure, not hardness.
